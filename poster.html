HEMOCONNECT

Next-Gen Blood Logistics & Clinical Coordination Platform

Java 21 • Spring Boot 3 Firestore & In-Memory Mock OWASP Hardened HemoAI Clinical Triage

MISSION & VALUE PROPOSITION

Eliminating critical blood discovery latency during medical emergencies. By combining smart immunohematology compatibility logic, Haversine spherical proximity routing, and a hybrid national donor registry scraper, HemoConnect bridges isolated blood bank reserves and voluntary community donors into an instantaneous life-saving dispatch grid.

domain Business Applications
  • 01.
    Hospital Blood Bank ERP (B2B) Real-time depletion monitoring, multi-ward requisitioning, and hospital access keys (HAK).
  • 02.
    Emergency Broadcast Grid (B2G) Rapid public health dispatch during regional disasters, trauma, or maternal hemorrhage codes.
  • 03.
    Zero Cold-Start Aggregation Overcomes sparse community launch data by scraping national voluntary registries on demand.
  • 04.
    Clinical Governance & Compliance Automates donor rest periods (90-day intervals) and guarantees 0% cross-contamination risk.
