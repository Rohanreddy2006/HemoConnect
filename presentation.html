01. PROBLEM STATEMENT THE CLINICAL CRISIS

The "Golden Hour" Blood Discovery Crisis

In life-or-death emergencies, delayed blood transfusion is a leading cause of preventable mortality.

CRITICAL DELAYS
The 2–4 Hour Search Lag

Families are forced to make panic-driven phone calls and travel between clinics without real-time inventory visibility.

FRAGMENTED SILOS
Isolated Blood Banks

Nearby hospitals have surplus units, but disconnected systems prevent inter-hospital sharing and coordination.

SAFETY & FATIGUE
Biological Mismatch Risks

Manual crossmatching risks fatal ABO/Rh mismatches, while frequent donors are called without mandatory rest intervals.

KEY STATISTICAL REALITY
Up to 70% of preventable trauma deaths are linked to transfusion delays beyond the first critical hour.
